Approvals

Reviewers' and editors' verdicts wait here. Nothing is written into the dataset until you approve a subject: its accepted labels then become reviewed (status 2) in Subject_info, and an editor's correction replaces the dataset's segmentation, which is backed up first. Tick the subjects listed, then approve them, close them, or send them to the reviewers or the editors, each time with a remark for their Subject_info if you like. A subject a reviewer rejected as a whole — a poor or cropped image, an implant — waits here too: close it with a remark saying why, which is all that is written into its Subject_info.
